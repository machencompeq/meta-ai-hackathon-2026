## 4. 繳交表單草稿（英文，送出前請使用者確認）

- **Name**：Paycheck Committee
- **One-liner**：A finance agent for people living paycheck to paycheck: it checks every bill gets paid, then lets a committee of Meta-model agents debate how much to invest — with a rule-based risk officer that can veto them.
- **Problem**：Students and young workers either miss bills or invest money they will need next week; robo-advisors ignore cash flow, and chatbots give unaccountable advice.
- **How**：Deterministic Python engine (day-by-day cash simulation, bills → emergency fund → invest) + config-driven agent committee (researchers, five investor personas, devil's advocate, chair) on Meta models; risk officer is code, not an LLM; every decision logged as JSON.
- **Meta models**：Muse Spark (chair, devil's advocate)；smaller Meta model / open weights (researchers, masters)
- **Track**：Indie Dev
